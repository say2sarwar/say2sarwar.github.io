About Me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at The University of Agriculture Swat.

My research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Multimedia Analysis, Multimodal Learning, Audio-Visual Understanding, and Adversarial Machine Learning.

I am pursuing doctoral research in Computer Science at National Chengchi University, Taipei, Taiwan. My research focuses on improving the robustness of deep learning systems for multimedia classification against adversarial attacks.

Alongside academic research and teaching, I work on AI and full-stack software systems, including intelligent applications, APIs, automation, healthcare information systems, and modern web platforms.
