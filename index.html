About

01

I'm a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at The University of Agriculture Swat, where I also serve as Head of Department — teaching, building the curriculum, and supervising undergraduate research.

My research centers on adversarial machine learning, computer vision, and multimedia analysis: how models classify images, audio, and video, and how they can be made to hold up under attack. I'm currently completing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at National Chengchi University, Taipei, where my dissertation explores adversarial defense in multimedia classification systems.

Outside the lab, I work as an AI and full-stack developer — building applications on large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and computer vision, and shipping them as real, working software rather than notebooks.
