About Me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at The University of Agriculture Swat, Pakistan, where I also serve as Head of Department.

My academic and research work focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Multimedia Analysis, Multimodal Learning, Audio-Visual Understanding, and Adversarial Machine Learning.

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at National Chengchi University, Taipei, Taiwan. My doctoral research focuses on improving the robustness of multimedia classification systems against adversarial attacks.

Alongside academic research and teaching, I have professional experience in AI development, full-stack software development, API integration, web technologies, healthcare information systems, and intelligent automation.
